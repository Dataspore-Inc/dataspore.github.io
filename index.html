Why Teams Get Stuck

Disconnected Tools Hurt Both Leadership Decisions and AI Projects

Most growing companies have great software that helps them understand their customers, their financials, and their business - but they rarely agree, instead living in several different islands - all saying something different.

Hours Lost to Spreadsheet Battles

Stripe knows who paid, HubSpot knows who your team talked to, and Zendesk knows who is frustrated. When those systems disagree, finance and operations spend hours every week reconciling CSVs by hand.

Expensive, Confused AI Assistants

Plugging AI tools directly into raw software accounts forces them to sift through massive, messy data files—driving up AI usage bills, slowing down answers, and causing costly mistakes.

Blind Spots & Governance Risk

Without a central checkpoint, leaders have no clear visibility into what data their AI tools are reading, whether sensitive customer records are exposed, or what actions automated workflows take.
